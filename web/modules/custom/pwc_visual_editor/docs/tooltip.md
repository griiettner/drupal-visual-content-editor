# Tooltip

```html
<apw-button id="tooltip-example" label="Label"></apw-button>
<apw-tooltip target="#tooltip-example">
  Nature, is the natural, physical, material world or universe.
  It can refer to the phenomena of the physical world, and also to life in general.
</apw-tooltip>
````
